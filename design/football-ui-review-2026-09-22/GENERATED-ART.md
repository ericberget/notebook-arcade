## UI integration

The generated field sits behind live player marks, names and routes at 30% opacity on desktop and 25% on phones. Club images replace the eight current club SVG doodles, retaining legacy emblems for older saved seasons. White interiors blend into notebook paper with CSS multiply compositing.

Player cards and prospects show prominent overall ratings, live jersey numbers inside six-stroke Os, and darker closed outlines around the full attribute scale. Gameplay Os use smooth, lightly retraced ellipses; the offensive blocking-guide Ts have been removed because they overlapped defender Xs. No game physics or saved-season schema changed.
